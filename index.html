The part nobody put in the job description

You are the middleware.

Every document starts the same way. You re-explain who the audience is. What tone we use. The three words we never use. That the executive summary runs to one page, not three. That sources get cited inline. That the divider slides in the template are not slide titles.

You explain it to one model. Then you explain it again to a different model because the first one wouldn’t open the file. Then you fix the output by hand, because it still put a 90-word paragraph in a box built for 25.

Your organization already knows all of this. It just has no way to tell the machine.
