Meet Geoff Pickering

Founder of GreenBrahma LLC. A senior growth and transformation operator you can bring in when something important needs to move.

“I built GreenBrahma around a simple belief: the hardest business problems rarely fit neatly inside one department.”

My career has moved across growth strategy, brand, media, digital transformation, SaaS products, customer experience, AI, analytics, sales and operations. That range has allowed me to see how the pieces influence one another, and where organizations lose momentum when they don’t.

I’ve led teams ranging from focused multidisciplinary groups to organizations and agency ecosystems exceeding 250 people, guided multiple SaaS products through different stages of development and worked across global markets and executive leadership environments.

GreenBrahma puts that experience directly into the room with the client.

How I work

Think at enterprise altitude. Operate at implementation depth.

The plan and the detail of carrying it out get the same attention, from the same person.

Connect what is usually kept apart.

Growth, customer experience, technology and economics are treated as one problem, because customers and P&Ls experience them that way.

Build capability inside your organization.

The aim is a team that can run the system after the engagement ends, with more than a set of recommendations to show for it.

Use AI where it creates operating leverage.

AI earns its place when it changes how work gets done. Being fashionable is not a reason.
