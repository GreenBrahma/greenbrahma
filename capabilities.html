Business problems first. Disciplines second.

GreenBrahma helps organizations turn growth strategy, marketing, technology and AI into executable business systems. The work is organized around four outcomes.

Growth & market activation

Turn strategic ambition into an executable growth plan.

Growth rarely stalls because an organization lacks ideas. It stalls when strategy, ownership, economics and execution do not line up. GreenBrahma helps leadership teams identify where growth can come from, determine what must change to capture it and create the operating plan required to make it happen.

Strategic planning, P&L stewardship, market expansion, competitive analysis, brand strategy, global and local activation, commercial planning, pricing, partnerships, business development.

Marketing, media & customer growth

Connect brand, media, customer experience and sales around one growth system.

Customers do not experience your organization by department. They experience one company. GreenBrahma connects brand, media, CRM, customer lifecycle, digital experience and sales so every interaction contributes to the same commercial objective.

Omnichannel and offline media, digital acquisition, paid search, SEO, social, content, CRM, marketing automation, customer lifecycle management, analytics, sales enablement.

AI, data & intelligent operations

Move AI from experimentation into the way work gets done.

GreenBrahma helps organizations determine where AI belongs, redesign the workflow around it and build the operating discipline required for adoption. The objective is not another proof of concept. It is measurable operating leverage.

AI readiness, use-case prioritization, workflow redesign, agentic workflows, context engineering, enterprise AI adoption, AI-enabled analytics, customer-facing agents, change and adoption programs.

Digital products & business transformation

Build the platforms, products and operating capabilities required for the next version of the business.

From proof of concept through MVP and market-ready product, GreenBrahma helps organizations evaluate opportunities, design the commercial model and connect product development with the people who ultimately have to sell, operate and use it.

SaaS product strategy, MVP development, product-market validation, digital and commerce transformation, product pricing, sales models, change leadership, data-enabled products, organizational planning.

Executive advisory

Sometimes the problem doesn’t need another project. It needs an experienced operator in the room.

Counsel for CEOs, CMOs and CROs, fractional executive leadership, board advisory, transformation governance, strategic workshops, vendor evaluation and major initiative reviews.

How we work

Four ways to bring GreenBrahma into the organization. Each is scoped to the problem, so pricing follows scope and is settled in conversation.

Strategic sprint

Focused question. Senior attention. Actionable answer.

A focused engagement addressing one important issue.

For example: a growth opportunity assessment, AI opportunity map, customer lifecycle audit, go-to-market reset or digital product viability review.

Transformation engagement

Design the change, then help carry it out.

A defined, multi-phase initiative in which GreenBrahma helps design and execute change.

Scoped by phase, with clear ownership on both sides at each one.

Fractional executive

Senior leadership without a permanent seat.

Embedded leadership for organizations that need senior marketing, growth, transformation or AI leadership without creating a permanent executive role.

Sized to the mandate: the role you need filled and the time it takes.

Executive and board advisor

An experienced operator in the room.

Ongoing strategic counsel for CEOs, founders, leadership teams and boards.

Useful for major initiative reviews, vendor evaluation, strategic workshops and transformation governance.
