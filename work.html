Selected work

Much of this experience belongs to organizations whose details are confidential. Each example is described by its situation, mandate, approach and what changed. No client names, and no numbers that can’t be shared.

Building an enterprise customer activation model

The situation
Customer data, media, CRM and market execution needed to operate as a coordinated lifecycle system.
The mandate
Lead cross-functional integration spanning media, customer segmentation, customer experience and marketing technology.
The approach
Create a lifecycle framework linking audience segmentation, channel activation and customer communications.
What changed
A repeatable framework existed for orchestrating customer engagement across marketing channels.
Why it matters
Most organizations already own the pieces. The work is making them run as one system that someone is accountable for.

Carrying SaaS products from concept toward market

The situation
Several software products sat at different stages of maturity, from proof of concept to market-ready, each needing more than a build plan.
The mandate
Lead five SaaS product teams across those stages.
The approach
Evaluate each opportunity, design its commercial model and connect product development with the people who would have to sell, operate and use it.
What changed
Multiple SaaS products were guided through different stages of development, from proof of concept toward market-ready.
Why it matters
A product that ships without a way to price, sell and support it is a cost. The commercial model belongs in the first conversation.

Taking one strategy into many markets

The situation
A growth strategy had to land in global markets with different customers, channels and teams.
The mandate
Lead global activation: the strategy, content and digital support that market teams use to execute locally.
The approach
Set strategy, content and digital support once, centrally, and leave campaign execution with the teams closest to each market’s customers.
What changed
Activation ran as a defined service to the markets, with a repeatable way to take a plan from strategy into local execution.
Why it matters
Global plans fail in the handoff to local teams. Activation is a discipline you can build, staff and repeat.
